# Банк фраз

[← Зміст](00_README.md)

Фрази нижче — **зразки змісту, а не обов'язковий текст.** Варіюй формулювання — система зараховує синоніми. Дзвінки українською/російською оцінюються за змістом.

## Привітання

**Вхідний — зараховується будь-яке:** Good morning / afternoon / evening · Hello · Hi · Hey there · Thank you for calling · Thanks for calling Freitty · How can I help you? · How may I help you? · What can I do for you? · This is [name] · будь-яка комбінація.

Ідеально:
> *«Thank you for calling Freitty, this is [NAME]. How can I help you today?»*
> *«Thanks for calling Freitty, this is [NAME]. What can I do for you?»*
> *«Hi, this is [NAME] with Freitty. How may I assist you?»*

Половина бала: «Yes sir, what location?» — робоче, але без привітання.
FAIL: одразу «What location are you interested in?»

**Вихідний — три кроки:**
> *«Hi, this is [NAME] from Freitty, the cross-docking platform.»*
> *«Am I speaking with [NAME]?»* / *«Is this [COMPANY]?»* / *«Who am I speaking with?»*
> *«Is now a good time?»*
> Не зараз: *«No problem at all. When would be a good time to call you back?»*
> Далі: *«I'm calling about [причина]. I just have a couple of quick questions for you.»*

## Слухання і уточнення
> *«Mhm» · «Got it» · «Sure»* — під час потоку клієнта
> *«Perfect, got most of it. Just one thing — what size are the pallets?»*
> *«So just to make sure I got that right — [резюме]. Is that correct?»*
> *«Got it — so that's 10 pallets arriving Tuesday. And what size are the pallets?»*
> *«What company are you with and your MC number?»*
> *«How many pallets and what size?»*

## Вантаж
> *«Is the freight on pallets, or is it loose in the truck?»*
> *«And are they standard size pallets — around 48 by 40 inches — or bigger?»*
> 5+ перевізників: *«That's quite a few carriers. To make sure we don't miss anyone, can you email me the list with their MC numbers? Much faster that way.»*

## Hazmat
> *«And this is regular freight, correct? Not hazardous materials?»*
> *«You mentioned FAK — just to confirm, no hazardous materials?»*
> *«Is it something dangerous?»* · *«Anything flammable or chemical?»*
> *«…so it's not hazmat, correct?»*
> *«What class is it?»*
> *«Unfortunately, our facility doesn't accept that hazmat class. I apologize we couldn't help with this one. Is there anything else I can assist you with?»*

Повний довідник — [Hazmat](09_hazmat.md).

## Ціна
> *«Great! The rate is [PRICE] per pallet. Sound good?»*
> *«Our transport team is already working on your quote — you'll have it within the hour, sometimes faster.»*
> *«Once you get it, just reply 'confirm' — that's all we need.»*
> *«Send the photos to orders@freitty.com, and I'll reply with the price as soon as they come in.»*
> *«The quote I'll give you based on photos will be preliminary — the final price is confirmed when freight arrives. But it's usually very close.»*
> *«That's [PRICE] for [DATE]. Would you like me to book that now, or would you like some time to think about it?»*

## Admin
> *«Perfect. And can I grab your email? Our system sends the confirmation and invoice there.»*
> *«Excellent. And how will you be paying for this?»*
> *«Just so you know — we require prepayment for transport services. Once you confirm the quote, we'll send the invoice. Pickup is scheduled after payment.»*
> *«Perfect. We'll continue with your usual payment terms.»*
> *«Can I get your name and the best way to reach you back?»*
> *«Of course. Can I get your name and number so they can reach you directly?»*
> *«If you'd like to text or call directly, here's the number: [NUMBER].»*

## Підсумок
> *«So just to recap: cross-dock on Tuesday, 8 pallets coming in. You'll get the confirmation email in a few minutes.»*
> *«So we've got [FREIGHT] pickup from [LOCATION] on [DATE]. Quote coming within the hour — just reply to confirm.»*
> *«We'll be expecting your truck at [TIME].»*
> Channel Transfer — що, куди, що далі: *«Send the photos to orders@freitty.com, and I'll reply with the price as soon as they come in.»*

## Закриття — зараховується будь-яке
| Група | Приклади |
|---|---|
| Питання про потреби | Is there anything else I can help you with? · Anything else for you today? |
| Прощання | Thank you for choosing Freitty · Thanks for calling · Have a great day · Have a good one · Take care · Talk to you soon · Thank you, bye |
| Наступний крок із каналом | I'll text you · I'll call you right back · You'll get the email shortly · We'll reach out within 24 hours · Send it over and I'll get back to you |
| Підтвердження | Got it · Perfect, I have everything · Mm-hmm, thank you |

Половина бала: голе «Okay.» або «Bye.»

**Платформа (тільки після вдалого бронювання):**
> *«By the way — next time you can book directly online at freitty.com. Takes less than a minute.»*
> *«Next time feel free to book online — same pricing, instant confirmation.»*
> *«You can also place your next order on our website — no need to call.»*

## Завжди говоримо
| Фраза | Коли |
|---|---|
| «Absolutely!» / «Perfect!» | Підтвердження, що почули і готові |
| «Got it.» | Після отримання інформації |
| «No problem at all.» | Клієнт чогось не знає або вибачається |
| «Just to recap…» | Перед закриттям |
| «Is now a good time?» | Кожен вихідний |
| «By the way…» | Email, оплата |
| «You'll receive a confirmation email shortly.» | Закриття після бронювання |

## Не кажемо → кажемо

| ❌ Не кажемо | ✅ Кажемо | Де рахується |
|---|---|---|
| «I think we can…» / «Maybe…» | «Absolutely, we can!» | Скрипт: впевнена мова |
| «Let me check if we do that» | «Absolutely, we handle that all the time» | Скрипт: впевнена мова |
| «I don't know» | «Let me find out for you right away» | Скрипт: впевнена мова |
| «That's not my department» | «Let me connect you with the right person» | Манера: «важка» фраза |
| «You should have…» / «You need to…» | «What works best is…» / «We recommend…» | Манера: «важка» фраза |
| «Actually, that's not how it works» | М'яко направляй без виправлення | Манера: «важка» фраза |
| «I'll call you back later» | «You'll hear from us within the hour» | Скрипт: ціна (якщо без часу) |

### «Maybe» — тільки там, де це ввічливість
| ✅ Нормально | ❌ Невпевненість |
|---|---|
| «Maybe you can send me your email» | «Maybe we can do that» |
| «Maybe tomorrow morning works better for you?» | «Maybe around $50» |
| «If maybe your driver comes after six…» | «Maybe one hour before closing or maybe two» |
| «So maybe tomorrow, got it» | «I think we have a location there» |

Про те, що ти маєш знати — ціни, правила, чи робимо ми це — **кажи впевнено:** *«Please let us know at least two hours before closing»*, а не «maybe one hour or maybe two».

### Передзвон
❌ «I'll call you back later» · «We'll get back to you» · «Someone will call»
✅ «I'll call you right back once the photos come in» · «within the hour» · «in about 20 minutes» · «after you send the BOL»

## Емпатія — будь-яким способом
- Словами: *I understand · I'm sorry to hear that · I apologize · I get that · that's frustrating · I can see why*
- Possessive: *your freight · your truck · your booking · your driver*
- Дією: *I'll move fast on this for you · let me take care of it · I'll handle it right now*
- Рівним тоном з агресивним клієнтом

## Спецситуації
**Пропущений:** *«We had a missed call from this number. Were you trying to reach Freitty, the cross-docking platform?»* → ні: *«No problem at all. If you ever need us, we're here. Have a great day!»*

**YELLOW callback:** *«I'm calling about your booking request, order FR[NUMBER]. I have a couple of quick questions to get you confirmed.»* → *«It's your [SERVICE] request at our [LOCATION] facility for [DATE].»*
Не кажемо: «flagged», «there's an issue», «there's a problem», «your request was rejected».

**Follow-up:**
> *«I'm calling back about your order FR[NUMBER] — I have an update for you.»*
> *«Just wanted to confirm everything is still on track for [DATE] at [TIME].»*
> *«We have you scheduled for [TIME] — just checking if you're on your way or if we need to adjust the appointment.»*

**Rework, причина пошкодження:**
> *«I understand you're looking for that. But we're not able to make any statements about the cause — we're a service platform, not an expert bureau. What we can do is fix the issue and document the current condition.»*

**Поліція / юристи:**
> *«For formal requests, we require a written submission. Can you provide your badge/bar number and agency?»*
> *«I'm not able to confirm or deny any records over the phone.»*

**Водій без верифікації:**
> *«Can I get your name and the company that booked this shipment?»*

**Партнер:**
> *«I'll pass your details to our partnerships manager — he'll reach out within 24 to 48 hours.»*
